About

Ashish Koshy Sabu, a multidisciplinary designer in training.

I’m a UX and interaction design student who treats design, technology and human experience as one subject.

Ashish Koshy Sabu
AKS Manipal University Jaipur

I study at Manipal University Jaipur, where I work across UX/UI, product design and 3D. I like projects that begin with a question about people and end with something you can touch, move through or play with.

Outside coursework I help lead a digital arts community and try new tools to see what they can do. I’m creative, curious, experimental and detail-oriented, and I sweat the small things that make an experience feel considered.

Design philosophy03

  • Start with people

    Good design begins with how someone feels at the moment of use, so I observe and ask before I draw.

  • Experiment openly

    3D, motion and code are part of my sketchbook. Playing with them early leads to better ideas.

  • Sweat the details

    Spacing, timing and tone decide whether something feels considered or only finished.
